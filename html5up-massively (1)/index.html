AI Automation Projects


VoltEdge Electronics: Online Store (Front End) Tools: Custom web storefront connected to n8n automation.

Home page (top), then catalogue, product listings and accessories (below)

VoltEdge Electronics is an online electronics store I designed to show how a customer-facing website connects to business automation. The home page introduces the brand and a featured product with clear calls to action. The catalogue organises laptops, smartphones, gaming consoles, audio, TVs and accessories with prices clearly shown in Nigerian naira, and every product has a quantity selector and an Add to Cart button so customers can review their total before checkout. Each order placed on the site feeds directly into the automated workflow described next.

Automated Order Processing Workflow (n8n)


Tools: n8n | Webhook | Gmail | Telegram Bot

Workflow (top), customer confirmation email (left) and instant team alerts on Telegram (right)

This workflow runs the moment a customer places an order. The website sends the order to an n8n webhook, the data is cleaned and structured, and two things happen automatically: the customer receives a confirmation email, and the team receives an instant Telegram alert. Each message carries the order reference, customer details, order total, the 5% store-credit bonus and the payment status. The bonus and payment stay marked as pending until payment is verified, which protects the business from rewarding unpaid orders. The result is faster customer response, no manual copying of order details and a team that is notified in real time.

AI-Powered Automated Reporting Workflow (n8n)


Tools: n8n | Google Sheets | JavaScript | OpenAI | Gmail | Google Slides | Google Drive

This scheduled workflow produces reports without manual effort. It starts automatically on a timer, reads the latest data from Google Sheets and processes it with a JavaScript step. An OpenAI language model then writes a clear, business-friendly summary of the numbers. The summary is emailed through Gmail, the results are inserted into a Google Slides report template, and the finished file is exported from Google Drive for sharing. It saves hours of report writing each cycle and gives decision makers a consistent report on time, every time.
